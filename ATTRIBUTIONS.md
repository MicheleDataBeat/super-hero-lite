# Attributions

Super-Hero Lite stands on two external, MIT-licensed upstream projects and on
one ancestor project of its own. It orchestrates the upstreams' installations
and techniques; it does not vendor, copy, or modify either of them.

## Matt Pocock skills

Matt Pocock's [skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) project is
available under the [MIT License](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/LICENSE).
Its clarification, specification and ticketing techniques are what
`super-hero-core` reaches for when requirement uncertainty is the problem.

## Obra/Prime Radiant Superpowers

[Obra/Prime Radiant Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) is
available under the [MIT License](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/main/LICENSE).
Its execution discipline — isolation, test-first work, systematic debugging,
verification before a completion claim — is what `super-hero-core` reaches for
when execution risk is the problem.

## Super-Hero Workflow

Super-Hero Lite is a descendant of
[MicheleDataBeat/super-hero-workflow](https://github.com/MicheleDataBeat/super-hero-workflow),
which is available under the MIT License and shares this project's copyright
holder. Lite's publication guarantees, its host-distribution seam, and much of
its tested lifecycle and manifest implementation are derived from that project
at version `1.6.4`, commit `690cfdb2ad9827e75b0da7ed3307d9a01ee72ee7`. The
ancestor's copyright notice and licence terms are carried in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Lite is not a Git fork and inherits none of the ancestor's history. What was
inherited conceptually and what was deliberately removed is recorded in
[Lineage](docs/lineage.md).
